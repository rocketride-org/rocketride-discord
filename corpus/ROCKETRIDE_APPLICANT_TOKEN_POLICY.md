RocketRide Applicant App Challenge — Token Policy

Version 1 · Last Updated: 03SEP2026

As part of applying to RocketRide , you may be asked to build a pipeline using RocketRide . You can build it locally with the open-source software at no cost. If you would inclined , you can deploy your pipeline to our Cloud and run your application which we encourage because it gives you real hands-on experience with how the product works . R unning pipelines on Cloud requires t okens and RocketRide Cloud has no free tier. So that applying to work here never requires you to spend your own money, we provide a one-time grant of free t okens for your application project. This policy explains how that works.

1. What you get

500 free RocketRide Tokens , granted once, to build and run your application-challenge project on RocketRide Cloud.

Redeeming the grant unlocks the Pipeline Builder for your account, so you can design, run, and deploy your pipeline.

No credit card is required to redeem or to use the grant.

2. Who is eligible

We are RocketRide are looking for people to join our team that are bought into and passionate about our product and what we are building. This challenge is to help us identify those people who understand what we are all about. The grant is available to applicants who have passed RocketRide’s initial screening and are building a project as part of the RocketRide application process. It is a non-commercial evaluation credit for that purpose , it is provided so you can demonstrate your knowledge of the product during your application, not for production or business use.

3. How to redeem

You do not need a paid subscription to redeem. The steps are:

Create a free RocketRide account (or sign in to one you already have).

On the application launcher, find the “Have a promo code?” field, enter your redemption code, and select Redeem . Codes may also be provided as a QR code that links to redemption.

Your 500 Tokens are added to your account and the Pipeline Builder unlocks.

Each redemption code is single-use and is tied to the account that redeems it. The grant is limited to one per person . Codes are not transferable and may not be sold, shared, or combined with other grants.

4. Expiration

Your 500 Tokens expire 30 days after the date you redeem the code. Any Tokens remaining after 30 days are forfeited and have no cash value. Redeem your code only when you are ready to start, so the 30-day window covers your build.

5. Using the Tokens

The Tokens work like any other RocketRide Cloud Tokens for running your project: pipelines consume Tokens when they execute.

Pipelines run only while you have Tokens. Because RocketRide Cloud has no free tier, once your Token balance reaches zero your pipelines will stop executing until you add more Tokens.

You are welcome to build locally with the open-source software instead of, or in addition to, using the Cloud grant. Although using the Cloud is optional we believe that deploying your pipeline gives you a deeper understanding of the product.

6. If you want to keep going

Whatever you build stays on your account. If you want to keep your application running after your free t okens are used up or expire, you can subscribe to a paid plan. Subscribing, and any optional features such as auto top-up, require a credit card and are billed to you . It is entirely your choice and are never turned on automatically. The free grant will never charge you and never requires payment details.

7. General

Free Tokens have no cash value and are not refundable, exchangeable, or transferable.

We may modify or end this program, or change the grant amount, at any time; changes do not affect Tokens already redeemed.

For a project that is especially original or impressive, RocketRide reviewers may, in their sole discretion, grant additional Tokens. These additional grants are entirely at our discretion and cannot be requested ; if we decide to provide more Tokens, we will let you know; please do not contact us to ask for them. Any additional Tokens are subject to the same terms, including that they expire 30 days after they are issued and have no cash value.

Attempting to obtain more than one grant , for example, by creating multiple accounts or submitting multiple applications to farm Tokens may result in forfeiture of the Tokens and closure of the accounts involved.

Use of RocketRide Cloud under this grant is also subject to the RocketRide Cloud Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

8. Where this lives

This policy will be published at https://cloud.rocketride.ai/careers/tokenpolicy and linked to job descriptions for applicants .
